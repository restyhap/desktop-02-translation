# OpenFlight — Logo Philosophy

## Philosophy

Translation carries meaning across boundaries — languages, cultures, minds. The mark is a monogram of two letters, D and T, drawn as **mountain and wing** — meaning is lifted from the source and flown to the shore.

**D = Mountain, the Source** — A single sweeping ridge, drawn in white on deep indigo. It is the weight of understanding: knowledge grounded, familiar, solid.

**T = Wing, the Crossing** — The horizontal bar arcs skyward, the body drops as a clean vertical. It carries the meaning across the gap. The gap is deliberate: source and destination are separate, and translation is the flight between them.

**Color and Material** — Deep indigo field (#1E1B4B) for depth and the night sky of thought. White for the source ridge (purity, the original text). One cyan gradient (#22D3EE) tips the wing — the sky at the moment of crossing, the single accent of the journey.

**The Signature Detail** — The wing lifts along the same curvature as the mountain's ridge. The shapes echo each other across the gap: what takes off from the source lands in the language of the destination. No translation is perfectly symmetric; the arc is the mark of carrying meaning faithfully.

## Manifesto

We do not build a logo that shouts. We build one that *lands*. Two marks, one ridge and one wing, separated by the flight they exist to make. Deep indigo holds the weight; the cyan tip is the moment of lift. This logo belongs on the shelf next to tools that matter.
